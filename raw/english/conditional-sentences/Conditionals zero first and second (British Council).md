# Conditionals: Zero, First and Second
Fuente: https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/conditionals-zero-first-second

## Definición y estructura básica

Las conditional sentences describen resultados basados en condiciones específicas. La estructura consiste en una cláusula "if" (la condición) y una cláusula principal (el resultado). El orden es flexible: "If you study hard, you will pass your exams" equivale a "You will pass your exams if you study hard."

## Zero Conditional

**Propósito:** enunciados universalmente verdaderos, leyes y reglas.

**Ejemplos:**
- "If I drink too much coffee, I can't sleep at night"
- "Ice melts if you heat it"
- "When the sun goes down, it gets dark"

**Estructura:** if/when + presente simple >> presente simple

## First Conditional

**Propósito:** situaciones futuras que se creen reales o probables.

**Ejemplos:**
- "If it doesn't rain tomorrow, we'll go to the beach"
- "Arsenal will be top of the league if they win"
- "When I finish work, I'll call you"

**Estructura:** if/when + presente simple >> will + infinitivo

**Alternativas comunes a "if":** unless, as long as, as soon as, in case.

**Ejemplos de alternativas:** "I'll leave as soon as the babysitter arrives"; "You can go to the party, as long as you're back by midnight"

## Second Conditional

**Propósito:** escenarios presentes/futuros imposibles o improbables.

**Ejemplos:**
- "If we had a garden, we could have a cat"
- "If I won a lot of money, I'd buy a big house"
- "I wouldn't worry if I were you"

**Estructura:** if + pasado simple >> would + infinitivo

**Nota especial sobre "be":** usar "were" después de "if" es gramaticalmente correcto ("if I were", "if he were"), aunque "was" aparece frecuentemente en inglés conversacional, particularmente con las formas he/she.
