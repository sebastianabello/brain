# Guía Completa sobre Adjetivos Comparativos en Inglés

Comparative adjectives establecen comparaciones entre dos entidades, destacando diferencias en cualidades o características.

## Reglas de formación

### Adjetivos cortos (una sílaba)
- Agregar -er: tall → taller, small → smaller, fast → faster
- Termina en -e: agregar solo -r: nice → nicer
- Consonante+vocal+consonante: duplicar consonante final: big → bigger, hot → hotter

### Adjetivos de dos sílabas
- Terminación en -y: cambiar y por i + -er: happy → happier, busy → busier
- Otros: usar "more"/"less": careful → more careful
- Excepciones: clever → cleverer/more clever

### Adjetivos largos (tres+ sílabas)
Siempre "more"/"less": expensive → more expensive, comfortable → less comfortable

## Errores comunes
1. Comparativos dobles: "more cheaper" ✗ → "cheaper" ✓
2. Than vs. From: "bigger from yours" ✗ → "bigger than yours" ✓ — siempre usar "than", nunca "from"
3. Formas irregulares: good → better; bad → worse; far → farther/further; little → less

## Ejemplos
- "The new office is brighter and more spacious than the old one"
- "This laptop is more expensive than that one, but it's faster"
- "The new restaurant is busier than the old one, but the food is better"
