# Formas de hablar del futuro en inglés

## 1. Present Simple (eventos programados)
"We have a lesson next Monday" / "The train arrives at 6.30 in the morning" / "The holidays start next week"

## 2. Present Continuous (planes/arreglos)
"I'm playing football tomorrow" / "They are coming to see us tomorrow" / "We're having a party at Christmas"

## 3. Will (creencias, ofertas, promesas)
Predicciones: "I think Brazil will win the World Cup"
Voluntad/disposición: "George says he will help us"
Ofertas y promesas: "I'll see you tomorrow"

## 4. Be Going To (planes e intenciones)
Intenciones previas: "I'm going to drive to work today"
Predicciones con evidencia: "Be careful! You are going to fall" (evidencia visible)

## 5. Will Be + -ing (acción continua en momento futuro)
Para algo ocurriendo antes y después de un tiempo específico: "I'll be working at eight o'clock" / "They'll be waiting for you when you arrive"

## 6. Verbos modales (may, might, could)
Incertidumbre: "I might stay at home tonight or I might go to the cinema"

## 7. Should (probabilidad)
Buena probabilidad: "We should be home in time for tea"

## Regla crítica: cláusulas temporales e if-
En cláusulas con "when, after, until" o "if", se usa presente para referirse al futuro:
- "I'll come home when I finish work" (no "will finish")
- "We won't be able to go out if it is raining" (no "will rain")
