# Cuantificadores en Inglés: Guía Completa

Quantifiers son determinantes que expresan cantidad o amount; preceden a sustantivos para indicar cuántos o cuánto de algo hay.

## Con sustantivos contables

Cantidad grande: many ("There are many students in this class"), a lot of ("She has a lot of friends")
Cantidad pequeña: a few ("I have a few friends coming over"), few ("Few students passed the difficult exam" — connotación negativa), several ("I've seen that movie several times")
Otros: a couple of ("a couple of books"), any (preguntas y negaciones)

## Con sustantivos incontables

Cantidad grande: much ("There is much traffic today"), a lot of ("There's a lot of snow outside")
Cantidad pequeña: a little ("I have a little money saved"), little ("There is little hope" — significado negativo), a bit of ("a bit of sugar")
Otros: any ("Is there any milk left?"), a great deal of (expresión formal)

## Matices críticos

### Few vs. A Few / Little vs. A Little
Few/Little (sin "a"): cantidad insuficiente, connotación negativa.
A few/A little (con "a"): cantidad positiva, accesible.

### Sustantivos duales
Algunos sustantivos funcionan como contables e incontables según contexto: coffee (incontable) "I drink a lot of coffee" vs. coffee (contable) "I ordered two coffees" (tazas).

## Errores frecuentes
1. "Many rice" → debe ser "much rice" (incontable)
2. "We don't have some dresses" → debe ser "any dresses"
3. Pluralización de incontables: "waters" es incorrecto

## Regla universal
"A lot of" y "lots of" funcionan tanto con contables como incontables — alternativas seguras cuando hay duda.
