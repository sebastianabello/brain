# Palabras Confusas: Adjetivos y Adverbios en Inglés

## Definición fundamental
Adjectives describen sustantivos; adverbs describen verbos. Muchos adverbios se forman añadiendo "-ly" a adjetivos, con excepciones.

## Ejemplo regla general: easy/easily
"The English test was easy" (adjetivo describiendo el sustantivo "test") vs. "I easily finished the English test in 45 minutes" (adverbio describiendo la acción "finished").

## Casos especiales

### Late / Lately
Late: adjetivo o adverbio, "after the correct time" — "a late lunch" (adj.) / "He slept late" (adv.)
Lately: adverbio, "recently" — "I haven't studied a lot lately"

### Hard / Hardly
Hard (adjetivo): "difficult" o "opposite of soft"
Hard (adverbio): "intensely" — "working hard"
Hardly: "almost not" — completamente distinto — "I can hardly hear you"

### Most / Mostly / Most of
Most / Most of (adjetivo): "the majority, the greater part" — "Most people like..."
Most (adverbio): "to the greatest degree", para superlativas — "most popular"
Mostly (adverbio): "mainly, generally, usually" — "We mostly go to dance clubs"
