# Parallel Structure
Fuente: https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/mechanics/parallel_structure.html

## Definición central

Parallel structure significa usar el mismo patrón de palabras para mostrar que dos o más ideas tienen el mismo nivel de importancia. Este concepto aplica a nivel de palabra, frase y cláusula, típicamente conectado con conjunciones coordinantes como "and" u "or".

## Estructuras paralelas en distintos niveles

### Palabras y frases con gerundios (-ing)
Correcto: *Mary likes hiking, swimming, and bicycling.*

### Frases de infinitivo
Correcto: *Mary likes to hike, to swim, and to ride a bicycle.* (alternativa: "to" puede aparecer solo antes del primer verbo)

## Errores comunes y correcciones

### Error tipo 1: Mezclar formas verbales
Incorrecto: *Mary likes hiking, swimming, and to ride a bicycle.*
Correcto: *Mary likes hiking, swimming, and riding a bicycle.*

### Error tipo 2: Patrones descriptivos inconsistentes
Incorrecto: *The production manager wrote his report quickly, accurately, and in a detailed manner.*
Correcto: *The production manager wrote his report quickly, accurately, and thoroughly.*

### Error tipo 3: Abandonar la estructura a mitad de oración
Incorrecto: *The teacher said that he was a poor student because he waited until the last minute to study for the exam, completed his lab problems in a careless manner, and his motivation was low.*
Correcto: reemplazar "his motivation was low" por "lacked motivation" para mantener el patrón de verbos conjugados en pasado.

## Paralelismo a nivel de cláusula

Al empezar con cláusulas, mantener ese patrón de forma consistente. Evitar cambiar de voz (activa a pasiva o viceversa).

Incorrecto (cambio de voz): *The salesman expected that he would present his product at the meeting, that he would have time to show his slide presentation, and that questions would be asked by prospective buyers.*
Correcto: usar voz activa consistentemente — *prospective buyers would ask him questions.*

## Listas tras dos puntos

Todos los elementos de la lista deben compartir la misma forma gramatical.

Incorrecto: *word meanings, pronunciations, correct spellings, and looking up irregular verbs.*
Correcto: *word meanings, pronunciations, correct spellings, and irregular verbs.*

## Estrategias de revisión

1. Pausar en las conjunciones coordinantes ("and"/"or") para verificar que los elementos son paralelos.
2. Organizar los elementos de una lista verticalmente para evaluar visualmente el paralelismo.
3. Escuchar los patrones sonoros y ritmos repetidos que indican consistencia.
