# Pronoun/Antecedent Agreement

Pronouns must agree with their antecedents (the nouns they refer to).

## Key rules
1. Neither, either, and each are singular.
2. Words ending in "one," "body," and "thing" are singular.
3. Collective nouns are singular when the group acts as a unit; plural when members act individually.

## Examples
- Each of the daughters followed (her, their) mother's footsteps. → singular
- Everyone complained that (his/her, their) food was cold. → singular
- Late in the evening, the jury finally reached (its, their) verdict. → singular (unit)
- Neither Clarissa nor Aleah wore (her, their) own dresses. → agreement with the nearer antecedent
- Either my brother or my uncles will fail to purchase (his, their) tickets. → agreement with the nearer antecedent
